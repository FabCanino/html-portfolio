My favorite movies

Movie List

Whiplash(2014)

A musical drama film. I love how intense the story is, which is clearly due to its focus on Neiman’s obsession with becoming the best jazz drummer, and how far we are willing to go to achieve our goals.

The Gentlemen(2019)

A comedy film with action and drama. It starts as a story being told by a narrator, something quite common in Guy Ritchie’s movies. We gradually discover the story through small flashbacks, and even when it seems like you have already figured out part of the plot, the narrator surprises you with an unexpected twist. Overall, it’s a very entertaining movie to watch.

Amelie(2001)

A French romantic comedy. I love the story and the characters. It’s a very charming and heartwarming movie with a touch of magic. The story can be confusing at first, but as the plot unfolds, you begin to understand that it is about someone’s search to be discovered.
